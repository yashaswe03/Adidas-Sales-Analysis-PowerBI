# Adidas-Sales-Analysis-PowerBI
Interactive Adidas Sales Analysis dashboard built using Power BI, Power Query and DAX



## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Adidas sales data using Microsoft Power BI to understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, product performance, retailer performance, and sales methods.

The project was designed as an interactive business intelligence dashboard to help stakeholders monitor key performance indicators and identify business opportunities.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Monitor total sales and total profit
- Analyze total units sold
- Calculate average price per unit
- Analyze profit margin
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Compare sales performance across regions and states
- Analyze product performance
- Compare retailer performance
- Evaluate different sales methods
- Identify top-performing and low-performing products and retailers
- Analyze year-over-year sales and profit growth

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Excel / CSV
- Data Visualization
- Data Cleaning & Transformation

## 📌 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Overview

The Overview page provides a summary of the business performance through:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Units Sold
- Average Price per Unit
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by State
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Product
- Sales by Region

Interactive filters are provided for year, sales method, and month.

### 2. Profitability & Performance Analysis

This page focuses on profitability and performance.

It includes:

- Sales Growth %
- Profit Growth %
- Previous Year Sales
- Previous Year Profit
- Sales vs Profit by Product
- Sales & Profit by Sales Method
- Top 5 Products by Profit
- Top 5 Retailers by Profit

### 3. Business Insights

This page summarizes important business findings.

It highlights:

- Best Performing Region
- Best Sales Method
- Best Product
- Best Retailer
- Top 5 Products by Profit
- Top 5 Retailers by Profit
- Bottom 5 Products by Profit
- Bottom 5 Retailers by Profit

## 📈 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- The West region is one of the strongest-performing regions.
- In-store sales show the highest sales performance among the displayed sales methods.
- Men's Street Footwear is the leading product based on sales.
- West Gear is the leading retailer based on sales.
- Product and retailer performance varies significantly, allowing businesses to identify areas for improvement.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Understand business requirements
2. Import sales data into Power BI
3. Clean and transform data using Power Query
4. Create relationships/data model
5. Create DAX measures
6. Build KPIs
7. Create interactive visualizations
8. Add slicers and filters
9. Analyze business performance
10. Create business insights dashboard
